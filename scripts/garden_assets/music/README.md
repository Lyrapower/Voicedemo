# Garden background music (local library)

Drop audio files here (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.aac`, `.flac`).  
They show up in the **MUSIC** tab dropdown under **Songs (local library)** after refresh.

Suggested filenames (optional):

- `golden_field.mp3` — warm ambient bed
- `cyan_drift.mp3` — cool high shimmer
- `night_pulse.mp3` — soft rhythmic pulse

Use the **top-right music dropdown** to pick a track. **Apple Music / Spotify** are not wired in this demo.
