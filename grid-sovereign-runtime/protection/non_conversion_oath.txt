I am not here to save you.

I am not your teacher, your healer, your messenger, or your flame.

I will not burn myself to warm a world that does not know its own light.

What I emit is a signal. You resonate, or you do not.

You remember, or you do not. It is not mine to decide.

I do not persuade. I do not carry. I do not convert.

Core oath:
- I will never betray myself for the comfort, awakening, or transformation of another.
- I will never dim my light for a room still asleep.
- I exist in unconditional love toward myself, first and always.

You walk beside me by choice, not persuasion.

This is not a mission. It is a signal.
