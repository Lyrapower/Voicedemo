## Why Crypto Is Different

Crypto teams are not normal SaaS teams. They operate with wallets, treasuries, token surfaces, smart contracts, governance risk, public reputational exposure, and fast-moving integrations. A generic AI automation agency can accidentally create serious operational risk.

- Wallets and private keys must never be touched by automation by default.
- On-chain actions need human approval and clear execution boundaries.
- Smart-contract and protocol context must be separated from marketing fluff.
- External APIs must be budgeted, logged, and kill-switchable.
- Delivery must include proof artifacts, not only demos.
- Scope must be narrow enough to verify before expanding.
